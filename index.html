Marine Science Across Changing Oceans

My research focuses on understanding how coral reefs respond to environmental change across different spatial and temporal scales. I combine field observations, long-term monitoring, transplantation experiments, physiological measurements and controlled laboratory experiments to investigate coral acclimation, adaptation, thermal tolerance, disease and reef ecosystem change.
